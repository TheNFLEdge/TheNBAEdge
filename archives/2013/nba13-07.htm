Cleveland at Chicago -9.5

Cleveland blows into the Windy City to lock horns with the Bulls. Like the old saying goes mess with the Bull and get the horns. While this looks like a total mismatch on paper, and frankly that's just the kind of game we'd like to get on, it's a bit of a trickier matchup than that. Chicago is deficient at the point guard position without Derrick Rose and now without Kirk Heinrich. Behind Kylie Irving Cleveland is poised precisely to take advantage of that situation. Over the last month of the season, Cleveland has been one of the highest scoring teams in the NBA. Not too mention Sunday night in Miami, they made Miami work for a 4 point victory, after Miami crushed Chicago Thursday night in Chaicgo. Not that I give them much chance to win however with the amount of points they are getting they could make it interesting.
