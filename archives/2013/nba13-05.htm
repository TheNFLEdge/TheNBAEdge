Portland at Miami -10.5

Portland blazes into Miami for a date with the Red Hot Heat. Portland is finishing up a 5 game road trip where they have managed only 1 win. Portland will be looking to add to that in a very tough place to come away with a win. Miami has only 3 losses at home on the season. Miami is now rolling with a 5 game winning streak and have won 10 of the last 12 games. this is the last home game for Miami before they embark on a 5 game road swing. Miami have smashed the two LA teams in thier last couple home games. Miami is that a risky proposition otherwise failing to cover 13 out of 23 times at home. The reason is usually that they just don't get all that interested in a non-descript regular season game. Then they are always laying double digits like this one. That being said Miami does have a slight tendancy to play to their competition when not totally enthused.

Denver at Toronto +2.5

Denver drive on into Toronto 2 to tangle with a sometimes troubling Toronto team. Denver was 1 of the hottest teams in the league here having won 9 straight. 9 straight until they were upended in overtime in Boston on Sunday. Granted they are still on the same road swing and it's never really a good idea to lay points on the road, and maybe especially when your team is on the last legs of a long road stretch. Toronto has a respectable 15 wins in 26 games against the spread at home. Toronto have frankly heated up since the tempurature has come down a little bit. As of late Toronto have won their lat last 2 games and 3 of their last 5 games. Still it's a relatively small number, and those guys out in Vegas, well they know what they are doing. All things considered Denver should win this game, but I tell you on a cold wintery night in Toronto, with enough points anything is possible.

Sacramento at Memphis -9.5

Sacramento swings through Memphis in another one of those games where it looks a little too good to be true. Sacramento may be slumping this season, but still have managed 11 wins against the spread. Sacramento happen to have a 2 game winning streak rolling into this one and have covered in the last 3 straight games. Memphis had been rolling along until Phoenix upset them last week (and well you know how I feel when I get beat, and that one messed up a beautiful parlay!) since then Memphis has won their last 2 games. Another big point spread here in this one, but in this case it is actually warranted. Memphis has bested Sacramento twice already this season by 16 and 30 points respectively. There is absolutely no reason not to expect more of the same on Tuesday night.

Phoenix at Los Angeles -7.5

One more bonus pick for those of you still checking us out, and perhaps having clicked on one or more of our links. (hint, hint!) We give you Phoenix flying into L.A. We could quite possibly not even really like this game, it's just that we haven't picked the Lakers here in what seems like a very long time. This is the first game back in LA for the Lakers since closing out a 7 game road swing. The last few games didn't exactly go the way they had hoped for. You are going to get that when you play the likes of Miami, in Miami. LA actually looked pretty decent for most of that game on Sunday and actually look like they are starting to get a little mojo working. Phoenix has been a slight pain in my back-side having now messed up a couple of my parlays! (did I mention how I hate when I get beat!) As we noted previously when postulating Phoenix, they continue to play 500 basketball on the road. For sure Phoenix will not be intimidated by LA, having beaten the Lakers once already this season by 6 points in Phoenix less than 2 weeks ago. Therefore we figure this is the perfect platform for a bite of some prime cuts of Kobe beef.
