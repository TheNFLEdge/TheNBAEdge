Atlanta at Indiana -3.5

So if you are one of those fools who dropped a big stack on the SuperBowl the other night, then you must be new around here, cause you would know that we absolutely DID NOT like that game at all. You are however in the right place to look to get it back. We like taking our shots on games were we can find a little edge in our favor. Our first stop tonight is a game we're not really crazy about, but one we think we can get an edge on. Atlanta heads on up to Market Square Arena where Indiana has been completely dominant on their home floor. Putting up 18 wins and only 3 losses. Atlanta is a couple games under .500 yet, at 10 covers in 22 road games are a respectable slightly under 50% road dog. See we look to find those lopsided easy blow outs, and there are enough of my computer simulations where it just happens to go that way, that I gotta take notice. Those are the exact kind of games that we'd like to find. The tricky thing giving me pause is that Indiana is on the second night of a back to back after battling a physical Chicago rival last night. While a fresh rested Atlanta team swoops in looking to steal another 1 on the road. As they somehow have been known to do against Indiana, whom Atlanta has beaten in 7 of their last 9 meetings. That is a big reason why you only have to lay about 3 points here, but also indicative of a match up advantage. This rendition of Atlanta has been struggling of late on the road having lost 7 of their last 8 road games. While Indiana has won and covered your last 14 straight at home. That right there is why the computer generates a scenario where Indiana blows them out in about 73% of the time, while the other 26% Atlanta clips them in a close one.
*********************************************************
IND 99 - ATL 89

LA Lakers at Brooklyn -4.5

Say what you will about Jay Z, or Kris Humphries, or the new look Nets, Brooklyn has been ballin big time at home this year. Now the against the spread record leaves something to be desired, and here in this last snap of 5 games have only covered once. Although to be fair a few of those games came to the likes of Chicago, Miami, Memphis and Houston. Brooklyn has still managed to come out on top in 10 of their last 11 home games, spreads aside. It will be a huge event Tuesday night that the Lakers are coming into town. L.A. have seemingly regained there footing and have been victorious in 5 of their last 6 games. Still, Los Angeles is in a midst of a 7 game road swing, and while they have won their last two the margin of victory has gotten smaller. They only narrowly escaped Detroit with a one point victory and failing to cover in 7 of their previous 10 games as well. Indeed we like Jay Z and his illuminated bunch to shine the way to the pot of gold for us as well.
*********************************************************
BKN 109 - LAL 94

Phoenix at Memphis -8.5

Lastly we pay a visit to Memphis, where despite management trying their best to wreck this team they just keep on winning. Memphis has posted 30 victories on the season with 16 of those coveted covers at home. Phoenix has been a pesky road dog however, having a managed to split the spread in the 24 road games, most typically as a road underdog. This being the biggest point spread on the night might give me some cause for concern. Yet still I like this game far more than that Superbowl that was just played Sunday night, and a little more than any of the other action on the slate for this evening, so why not join us in growl with the Grizz.
*********************************************************
MEM 110 - PHX 96
