GET THE NCCA BETTING LINE

After last night's action the old Big East is threatening to take over half of the Final Four. With another number 1 seed going down last night and another big upset as well, it is almost a certainty that bettors will be hesitant to lay the points on a favorite. Which of course plays perfectly into our hands. Oregon is a very, very good team. They are clearly much better than a 12 seed. With bettors jumping all over the points, we will wind up getting an even better spread after last night. For a Louisville team that has been winning by margins of close to 30 points. Oregon has had a nice run, but only 1 team can dance on, Louisville.

GET THE NCCA BETTING LINE

Not the strongest play on our board, and the computer projects this one at a margin that we normally would not even consider. Michigan and the Big 10 have proven that they are among the best conferences in the tournament. While the Big XII (or whatever they call Kansas conference) has been an abject failure thus far outside of Kansas.
