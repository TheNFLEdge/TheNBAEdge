THU, MAR. 28

Marquette vs. Miami -3.5

While the Miami heat were busy running off 27 straight wins, there was another basketball team down at the bottom of the USA running off a few wins of their own. The Miami Hurricanes, who ran the table in the ACC tournament, and have now put together two straight NCAA wins. They face off against Marquette team in an epic sweet 16 match up. Marquette being the co-champions of the Big East, a tournament tested team that definitely has the size and toughness to match up with Miami. Miami is a senior laden team that has a chip on their shoulder as they won the ACC, you know the conference with Duke, and Carolina title, and the ACC tournament and were dismissed as a no. 2, seed. What Marquette don't have is an electric playmaker like Shane Larkin. Larkin may be the best point guard in the tournament, and while a tough long Big 10 team like Illinois schemed to take Larkin away, he still delivered defiantly in the clutch. Jimmy Laranaga, Jimmy from the Bronx, will ride his dynamic point guard to keep Miami dancing. You can bet on it.

THU, MAR. 28

LaSalle vs Wichita St. -3.5

This is one of those really tough match ups here, and if you read our OUR SPECIAL NCAA Tournament Issue # 2 you probably would know to stay away from a game like this one. LaSalle is one of those teams that you may not have ever heard of but all of a sudden here they are in the sweet sixteen, having knocked off a no. 3 seed Kansas St. and giant killers Mississippi along the way. LaSalle battled tournament tested teams like St. Louis, and Butler in the regular season, and are really underrated here as an underdog. Wichita is another of those unknown quantities, at least to the average Joe. Wichita State is one of those mid-major schools but from a conference that has posted many a tournament shocker. All Wichita has done is knock off a Pittsburgh team that Jim Boeheim of Syracuse said was the best in the Big East, and then follow that up by knocking out no. 1 seed Gonzaga. Wichita State is solid and can really shoot. LaSalle will try and spread the floor and knock down the three, and if their shots are going in they could be trouble, but if not, Wichita's size and fundamentals will make this a one sided affair. You know just the kind we LOVE to put our money on.

WIC 69 - LAS 61

THU, MAR. 28

Syracuse vs Indiana -6.5

A rematch of the classic 1987 National championship game. The one Keith Smart won with a buzzer beater at the end. The stakes are a little bit less for this one, but not by much. For our nightcap we feature our upset special. Well as close to an upset as we're going to pick. Syracuse plays the role of a underdog against number 1 seed Indiana. Much like in 1987, while Indiana is a good deserving number 1 seed, they did struggle in their last game with Temple pushing Indiana to the brink. The Syracuse zone defense is going to give Indiana all sorts of trouble scoring. Syracuse relies on that defense to create offense as they only have 1 legitimate scorer. In a straight half court game they probably don't stand a shot. I'm bettin' Indiana coming off a little lay off shoots a little cold in this one, creating opportunities for Syracuse to stay in the game and within the requisite 6 and a half points.
************************************
IND 61 - SYR 56
