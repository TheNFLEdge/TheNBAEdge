Toronto at Indiana -8.5

Toronto travels to Indiana where the pace has been too frantic for many an opponent. Indiana has a 17 game at home unbeaten streak running while Toronto is running to avoid the lottery. Now in the NBA even the worst teams can be dangerous with a big enough point spread, and Toronto is no different with 12 road covers, but 6 of those covers in the last 7 road games. Still Indiana has been one of our best picks consistently for a couple of years running now. They have actually been pounding people as of late. On a February Friday night in Indianapolis they are the only show in town and we are going to pound with them pound for pound into ground.
