It is with trepidation we have reached the end of our basketball season. Despite posting a nearly 80 percent against the spread record, it was a bit of a disappointment by our standards. After a couple of stellar seasons we came to expect better.

Now that the playoffs are well underway it is safe to soon assume that we won't be giving you any playoff picks. As is the case around here we avoid playoff wagering much as humanly possible. However this year's playoffs are a bit of an exception as there are a few colossal mismatches, but the prices will be rather steep. Instead look for some special circumstances were upset could happen. Memphis has a big team, Indiana is highly underrated and have been highly successful for us in the regular season, both will be underdogs getting points. Miami is a powerhouse and should be favored in every game, however, in particular spots could be vulnerable to point spreads, and covers. Proceede with due caution... and the best of luck to you.
