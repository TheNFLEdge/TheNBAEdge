Indiana at Orlando +9.5

Welcome back, where we look to for a fun filled Friday of free throws and fist pumps. Our first game once again is on the riskier side. However on a Friday night where we got almost half a dozen home dogs to choose from, we start off on the road to O-town. We look to our old friend Indiana, where we have gone several times in our matinee, mostly to good results. We like this one as Orlando by virtue of a Wednesday night wire to wire thriller with the champs as a 16.5 point underdog, on the heels of road win, we got us a streak! The NBA is all about momentum, and every team makes a run in the NBA. Indiana however is one of those beasts built to buttress against those runs. They are big and defend as well as anyone. Orlando won't have a huge inside advantage against Indy, like they did in Miami the other night. Indiana has won three of their last five games, and have recently dropped 2 of the last three at home. We are counting on all that factoring in to give us a favorable point spread. However keep in mind laying double digits on the road is often a recipe for disaster.

Atlanta at Boston -3.5

There are very many difficult games this week to choose from, as our esteemed colleague posted in the forum, you want to choose very wisely. After all it is quality instead of quantity. Though we do provide a fine quantity of winners. Our next game that we have a very high probability of hitting on takes us on up to Boston. Boston has been beaten up by the bookies, especially since the Rondo injury. In actuality since the injury Boston has been best bargain in basketball. Here again they are only a modest home favorite against Atlanta on Friday night. Atlanta can be a pesky road team to fade, they have 17 road covers in 31 road games, 55% if you are keeping score. Boston has been a moderately worse bet at home this season. Yet Boston have been money as of late, winners of the last 4 straight, and covering in 6 of the last 8 games. In a weekend of tough match choices, this one has one of the best shots of being right.
