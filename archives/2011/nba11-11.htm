BET THE CHALK!

Well it sounds corny, but when filling out your brackets the committee really knows it’s business, and upsets are rare! Not that they don’t happen, and when they do, they seriously overhype them looking for the next Cinderella story, but lets look at the numbers. First off NO #16 seed has EVER beaten a number one. Just the facts ma’am. This year they have added a little twist with two play-in games, no matter the winner will just get the right to get blown out by some heavyweight! The number 2 seeds have generally fared just as well. Except for a couple highly celebrated upsets most all number 2 seeds move on. So that right there is 8 winners for you, and a decent start to your brackets… in fact you can fill out most if not all of your bracket – at least the first round just by going with the higher seeded team, at least up until you get to the 5 seed. Even so, throwing in the no. 3 and no. 4 seeds, that now gives you 16 pretty good potential winners in your bracket!
 
 





The NCAA tournament started back in 1939, and 40 years later they started seeding teams. In 30+ years of seeding teams, only 3 teams seeded lower than no. 4 seed have ever won the Championship. Villanova as the 8 seed in 1985, was the lowest seeded team to win the tournament. Jim Valvano’s NC State team in 1983, and Danny Mannings Kansas team in 1988 both as no. 6 seeds were the only other and lowest seeded teams to cut down the nets. Going by that proven fact, you can pretty much scratch off 12 teams in each region, play-in games notwithstanding. In fact only 8 teams seeded higher than no. 6 have ever reached the final four! Taking an underdog, especially one lower than say a 5 seed, to win a region is the equivalent of a sucker bet.
