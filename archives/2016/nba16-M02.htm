Yale vs Duke -6.5

Another Cinderella in Yale who filled the bill beatifully, the Ivy league champs went and punched Big 12 big name Baylor right in the mouth. They step up in class here to a young, but very talented Duke team. This one could be a little nervewracking at first as a young Duke team may be overlooking Yale here, but this is no typical Ivy league team, as Baylor can attest to. So if you are fishing for upsets, you might look at Yale for the 1st half. However after coach K gets them in at halftime and finds the exploitable mismatches look for Duke to pull away in the 2nd half, and the final score won't indicate what kind of battle this one was.

Little Rock vs Iowa St -4.5

Another sweet Cinderella 12 seed upset winner, Arkansas Little Rock, run into Iowa State. Aside from the seedings, I really like Iowa State this year, and in this tournament. Iowa State has a loaded team featuring senior All American forward Georges Niang. Niang is capable of putting Iowa state on his back and carrying them through any game. Now the Sun Belt champions Little Rock were and are a great Cinderella team with only 4 losses on the year, although are stepping up in class here against another Big 12 tested Iowa State team. It will be close, but not as close as the oddsmakers predict, and down the stretch when Little Rock are forced to foul Iowa State will keep on knocking them down to clear the spread and the cash.

Butler vs Virginia -8.5

Alright, for those clamoring about how all we pick is chalk, here is one for you! The no. 1 seed ACC monsters Virginia battle the Butler Bulldogs. However Butlers is no ordinary 9 seed. After upsetting Texas Tech in the opening round Butler is no stranger to facing number one seeds, and in fact have knocked 2 other No. 1 seeds out of the NCAA Tournaments. Senior laden Virginia probably moves on, but Butler will battle them down to the wire. If Butler can stick around, who knows anything can happen in the final moments, and why they call it March Madness.
