THE NBA EDGE: 2014

Playoff Edition 2014

Its NBA playoff time and you know what that means. The end of the edge. The end of the regular season for the NBA edge.com. While this past season we have not been as prolific with our picks as in years past. That being said there's still plenty of good gets left for last. For the NBA Playoffs we'll depart with some of our hard earned wisdom earned from years of wagering on NBA playoff action. Hoping to help you get the edge. So the important thing to keep in mind for the NBA Playoffs is it's a series and not a game. That could be difficult to do having just watched Cinderella dance in the NCAA tournament just a few weeks ago. It can be awful tempting to try and take the hot underdog. Although to be honest that's the best way to make money on NBA Playoffs. If that's the sort of thing you're after. And if you're here reading our site ;-) wink, wink, nudge, nudge we know that's clearly the last thing on your mind. Seriously, consider which is the better team, over the course of the entire season, because while you can get an upset win in a game or two. During the grind of a seven game series invariably the better team winds up on top. Better team can also be a team that has been playing very well down the stretch matched up with a team that perhaps due to injuries or fatigue is NOT the same team they were in the regular season, or in previous seasons. This year's particularly tricky because I think, oh Miami is especially vulnerable.

However I don't know that Miami can be upset in the first round. I like what Brooklyn has done in the second half of the season with or without Kevin Garnett. Although Toronto can be somewhat of a matchup problem for them, Indiana surprisingly also seems extremely vulnerable. Despite earning the top seed. The West

The West was a little more grouped together in my opinion, and maybe more fertile ground seeking an upset. Personally, I would avoid picking against the likes of San Antonio regardless how old or tired you may think they are. Still, you do need to be mindful of the health of Tony Parker. The 2-7 match up with Oklahoma City might be a place you could look for an upset. Not so much as and indictment of Oklahoma, rather as a nod to Memphis. Memphis made it to the conference finals last year knocking out Oklahoma City along the way. Sure Oklahoma City was without Westbrook last year but I don't think that would made a difference. Memphis with their great size in inside, beat them up and dominated, and I don't see how that matchup is going to change this year. However with home court advantage, I wouldn't want to go against Kevin Durant in a _game 7_ scenario. Similarly to Indiana - Atlanta in the east, if you want to get on Memphis do it early on, or at home, and only wager silly play money if you feel a little crazy on Memphis for the series. In the end I think Oklahoma City moves on. Another darling from last years playoffs is Golden State, matched up against the 'other' Los Angeles team, the Clips. With the shooters on Golden State, they have shown that they can heat up and take anyone out on any given night, they were nice priced dogs for us last year, but we played them in individual games. I don't see this team as being able to catch lightning in a bottle once again, and will be taking a game to game approach even if they split the regular season series with L.A. I can't see them pulling that off again. Maybe they split the first 6 games, but L.A. with the home court for game 7 advances. Right and best of luck to ya!
