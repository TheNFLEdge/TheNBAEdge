The NCAA tournament started back in 1939, and 40 years later they started seeding teams. In 30+ years of seeding teams, only 3 teams seeded lower than no. 4 seed have ever won the Championship. Villanova as the 8 seed in 1985, was the lowest seeded team to win the tournament. Jim Valvano’s NC State team in 1983, and Danny Mannings Kansas team in 1988 both as no. 6 seeds were the only other and lowest seeded teams to cut down the nets. Going by that proven fact, you can pretty much scratch off 12 teams in each region, play-in games notwithstanding. In fact only 8 teams seeded higher than no. 6 have ever reached the final four! Taking an underdog, especially one lower than say a 5 seed, to win a region is the equivalent of a sucker bet.

WHERE TO FIND THE UPSETS

That all being said, this is the NCAA tournament, there are celebrated upsets every year, the Trick is to find them. Pretty much since the inception of this tournament there has been a 12 seed upset a number 5 seed every single year. Now there's a couple of reasons for that, As we just told you above, the top four teams rarely if ever lose. Since there are going to be upsets every year the best place to go and pick one, is a 12-5 match up. And who knows if the committee doesn't have a hand in perpetuating that by seeding overseeding some number 5 seeds against movies underrated 12 teams. Look especially for the big name conference program against A lesser renowned school with a quality body of work behind them, such as a mid-major champion. Whatever the reason there has been a 12-5 upset every year, and some years we might have more than one 12-5 upset. So you could look at it as your 5 pick might have only a 75 percent chance of advancement, and in any case wouldn't be wanting having them going very deep.
