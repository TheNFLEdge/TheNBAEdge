About The NBA Edge

Classic basketball picks, computer analysis and a new chapter for the Edge.

From the archives

The NBA Edge brought data-assisted basketball picks and commentary online in an earlier era of sports analytics. The preserved classic issues and record tables offer a look at those picks, results and the voice behind the site.

The 2015 season table reports 12–1 against the spread, or 92.31%, across four issues. The surviving issue #3 copies do not fully substantiate that total, and an alternate includes a college game. It is an archival report, not a verified NBA-only record or evidence of the new model's performance.

In memory of a friend

Chris “Green Bomber” Campbell

The original Three Ball was selected by Chris “Green Bomber” Campbell from the analytics' most confident plays. Following his passing in the summer of 2026, this new NBA Edge is dedicated to his memory.

His part in the story remains an important part of the Edge. We'll continue to share that story here.

A new pilot

For now, the automated site publishes all of its computer-generated game projections rather than a Three Ball selected by Chris, using Greggy the Greek's PanaGeoTech formula.

This season is a pilot for the automated model; historical hand-selected results are not a track record for the new model.

Projections are estimates, not guarantees. Point spreads and totals are snapshots and may be unavailable or change. For entertainment and informational purposes only.
